# Credibility Statement Decisions

**Author**: Upstream Copy Strategist
**Date**: 2026-05-31
**Status**: Approved (Strategist Deliverable)

This document provides three alternatives to the false `$2.4B+ Capital Tracked / 12,000+ Active Deals` statistics previously live on the homepage. It outlines the strategic reasoning for selecting the practitioner-led credibility model.

---

## The Three Alternatives

### Option 1: Founder-Credibility Framing (The Operator Guide)
> `"Built by active real estate operators who closed 40+ deals before writing a single line of code. We built the system we needed to survive our own flips and syndications."`
- **Focus**: Personal credentials, practitioner-to-practitioner empathy.
- **Frameworks**: Cialdini (Authority & Liking), StoryBrand (Guide).

### Option 2: Methodology-Credibility Framing (The Financial Standard)
> `"Calculations validated against standard real estate accounting metrics. Every IRR, Cap Rate, and cash-on-cash metric is computed using verified formulas you can hand directly to your CPA."`
- **Focus**: Technical accuracy, CPA hand-off safety.
- **Frameworks**: Made to Stick (Concrete & Credible).

### Option 3: Honest-Scale Framing (The Pilot Group)
> `"Currently running active pilot deals with a cohort of 48 local operators across 12 markets. Growing carefully to maintain high-touch support."`
- **Focus**: Small, elite pilot scaling, transparency.
- **Frameworks**: Cialdini (Scarcity & Social Proof).

---

## Selected Verdict: Option 1 (Founder-Credibility Framing)

We select **Option 1** as the canonical homepage credibility block.

### Why this converts (Strategic Reasoning):
1. **Empathy & Trust (Cialdini Liking)**: Real estate operators are deeply skeptical of general software. When they read "Built by active operators," their guard drops because the creators have "dirt on their boots."
2. **Authority as Guide (StoryBrand)**: Instead of positioning PaperWorking as a massive faceless SaaS corporation, it positions the founders as the guide who survived the exact spreadsheets chaos the user is currently experiencing.
3. **Unexpected Contrast (Made to Stick)**: On a web filled with hyped-up, fabricated metrics, stating that the founders closed 40+ deals ourselves is concrete, realistic, and highly memorable.
