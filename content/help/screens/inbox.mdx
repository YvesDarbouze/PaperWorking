# Inbox & Notifications

The Inbox dashboard aggregates critical action items and lifecycle alerts in one place.

## Notification Categories

- **Metric Alerts**: Warns you if a project's DSCR falls below 1.0, Occupancy falls below 80%, or Cash Flow becomes negative.
- **Lifecycle Events**: Triggers alerts for property tax due dates, lease renewals in 60 days, and insurance renewals.
- **Vendor Activity**: Shows notifications when a vendor logs quotes or updates a milestone checklist.
- **Team Updates**: Real-time notifications when team members modify financials or role configurations.
