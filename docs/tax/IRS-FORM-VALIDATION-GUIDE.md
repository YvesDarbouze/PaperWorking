## 2. Form 1040-ES Estimated Tax & Safe Harbor Rules

- **Quarterly Vouchers**: Vouchers 1 (Apr 15), 2 (Jun 15), 3 (Sep 15), 4 (Jan 15).
- **Safe Harbor Requirement**:
  - **AGI <= $150,000**: Pay 100% of prior year tax obligation divided equally across 4 quarters.
  - **AGI > $150,000**: Pay 110% of prior year tax obligation divided equally across 4 quarters.

---

## 3. Form 8825 Multi-Member LLC Allocations

- Partnership rental real estate income is reported on **Form 8825** attached to Form 1065.
- Income/expense items allocated according to Operating Agreement ownership percentages.
- K-1 Line 2 reflects net rental real estate income allocated per partner.

---

## 4. 1099 Filing Thresholds

- **Form 1099-NEC**: $600+ per non-corporate contractor per calendar year.
- **Form 1099-MISC**: $600+ rent paid to non-corporate landlords.
- **Form 1099-S**: Real estate sale proceeds (all sales, no minimum dollar threshold).
- **Warning Threshold**: Automated warnings generated when payments reach 90% of threshold ($540+).
