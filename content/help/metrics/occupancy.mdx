# Occupancy Rate

Occupancy Rate measures the ratio of occupied rental space relative to the total available inventory.

## Formula

```
Occupancy Rate = (Occupied Units / Total Units) * 100
```

## What this tells you

Occupancy shows property tenant health. Low occupancy reduces gross revenues while fixed expenses remain high, impacting NOI and Cash Flow.

## How to improve it

- **Offer Move-In Incentives**: Run temporary lease promotions or concessions to fill vacancies.
- **Improve Tenant Retention**: Respond quickly to maintenance requests and update older units.
