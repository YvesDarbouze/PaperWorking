## 3. Non-Resident Withholding Rules

- States such as **California, New York, and Hawaii (HARPTA)** enforce non-resident tax withholding on real estate sales or entity distributions.
- PaperWorking alerts investors when real estate entities distribute capital across state lines to non-resident partners.
