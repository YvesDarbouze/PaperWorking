## 4. Problems & Pain Points

- **Core challenge:** Investors run deals on spreadsheets, notes apps, and email threads. Critical numbers get lost, duplicated, or miscalculated — and they don't find out until closing day or tax time.
- **Why current solutions fall short:** Generic project management tools (Asana, Monday) don't understand deal phases, cost basis, or contingency deadlines. Excel works until you have 3+ deals or partners who need visibility.
- **What it costs them:**
  - Missed contingency deadlines → lost earnest money ($5k–$25k)
  - Inaccurate cost basis → overpaying capital gains tax
  - Slow reporting → LP distrust and lost syndication capital
  - Contractor disputes → delayed renovations, blown budgets
- **Emotional tension:** "Am I going to look stupid in front of my investors?" / "I know my numbers are wrong but I don't have time to fix them." / "I'm making money but I have no idea how much."

## 10. Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Direct, confident, practitioner-to-practitioner
- **Communication style:** Instructional where needed, persuasive where appropriate. Zero fluff.
- **Brand personality:** Expert, no-BS, builder-minded, experienced, trustworthy
- **Voice DO's:**
  - Use contractions (you'll, it's, don't)
  - Use "you" and "your" — talk to the reader, not about them
  - Be specific — cite dollar amounts, percentages, real scenarios
  - Vary sentence length — short punchy lines mixed with longer explanations
  - Start sentences with verbs when giving instructions ("Open the...", "Set your...")
- **Voice DON'T's:**
  - Don't hedge ("may help", "can potentially") — be direct
  - Don't use passive voice ("was created", "is ensured")
  - Don't use vague positives ("robust", "comprehensive", "powerful")
  - Don't open with "In today's..." or "Whether you're..."
  - Don't use "Navigate to" — say "Go to" or "Open"
  - Don't use "Absolutely" as a response opener

## 11. Style Guide

- **Grammar:** American English. Oxford comma. Contractions encouraged.
- **Capitalization:** Feature names capitalized (Command Center, Deep Focus, Engine Room, Document Vault). Generic terms lowercase (deal, project, investor).
- **Formatting:** Em dashes (—) for breaks. Bold for UI element names in instructions. Bullet lists for feature sets.
- **Preferred terminology:**
  - "Deal" not "investment property" or "asset"
  - "Exit" not "disposition" or "liquidation event"
  - "Burn rate" not "carrying cost velocity"
  - "Cost basis" not "adjusted tax basis" (unless in CPA context)
