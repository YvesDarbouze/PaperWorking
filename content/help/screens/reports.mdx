# Reports & Export Hub

The Reports page lets you compile financial summaries across single projects or your entire portfolio.

## Available Exports

- **Schedule E Preview**: Automatically maps transactions and operations expenses to standard Schedule E categories.
- **Profit & Loss (P&L)**: Renders property-specific net operating income and net income over chosen calendar years.
- **Tax Pack**: Combines all receipts, invoices, P&Ls, and depreciation schedules into one neat ZIP file for your CPA.
