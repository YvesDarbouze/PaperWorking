Platform Overview

## The Real Estate Investment Operating System

Most investors manage six- and seven-figure deals on spreadsheets that break when you add a column. PaperWorking replaces that mess with one system organized around how deals actually work: four phases, acquisition through exit, with every dollar tracked along the way.

1.Acquisition

#### The Capital Gateway

Source deals, generate offer letters, and collect capital commitments from your syndicate. No more toggling between CRMs, email threads, and shared drives to figure out where a deal stands.

2.Purchase

#### The Compliance Vault

Loan docs, attorney sign-offs, title commitments, and contingency deadlines live in one place. You stop chasing PDFs through email and start closing on time.

3.Hold

#### Margin Protection

Every day you hold a property, it costs you money. The platform tracks your daily burn rate down to the penny so you know exactly what each extra week on market does to your ROI.

4.Exit

#### Financial Reconciliation

Sale or refi, the platform pulls every cost from Phases 1 through 3 and calculates your actual ROI, IRR, and cash-on-cash return. Export a one-page deal summary or tax-ready CSV for your CPA.

### Not a project management tool. A risk mitigation platform.

Lost documents. Untracked holding costs. Last-minute closings where nobody can find the right version of the HUD-1. That's what happens when your deal management lives in five different places. PaperWorking puts it all under one roof so you can scale without the chaos. You're running a business. Your tools should reflect that.

Before You Commit

### No surprises. No fine print.

Do Vendors get access to my financial data?

No. Vendor accounts are structurally isolated. They can only see their marketplace listing, inbox messages, and work requests sent to them by REI Teams. They have zero visibility into any financial data, deal analytics, or capital stack information. Access is enforced at the database level, not just the UI.

Can I upgrade from Individual to Team later?

Yes, upgrades take effect immediately. When you upgrade, we prorate the remaining balance of your current billing period and apply it to the Team plan. All your existing deals, documents, and financial records stay intact. No data migration needed.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account starts with a full-access 14-day free trial. A credit card is collected at checkout, but you won't be charged until your trial ends. You get the complete Team-tier experience so you can evaluate every feature. At trial end, choose the plan that fits your workflow. If you don't convert, your data is preserved for 90 days in case you return.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account settings. You retain full access until the end of your billing period. No surprise charges, no penalty fees. Your data is preserved for 90 days, and you can export everything (CSV or PDF) at any time.

Is there volume pricing for large teams?

For organizations needing more than 10 seats or enterprise-grade compliance (SOC 2, SSO/SAML), contact our sales team. We offer custom pricing with dedicated onboarding, SLA guarantees, and white-glove migration assistance.
