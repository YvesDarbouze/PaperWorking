# Team Management & Roles

Invite partners, deal coordinators, and external professionals to collaborate on your projects with clear permissions boundaries.

## Roles Matrix

- **Admin**: Full access to deal files, organization settings, and Stripe billing.
- **Deal Lead**: Access to write/edit specific assigned deals, but cannot view billing.
- **Accountant**: Read-only access to ledger items, tax exports, and Schedule E categories.
- **Vendor/Contractor**: Interactive access to submit quotes, update project milestones, and request draws.
