# Version 1.0.0 — Official Release

We are excited to launch PaperWorking v1.0.0, our all-in-one real estate investment lifecycle platform.

## Added
- **Stripe Billing Integration**: Added support for Solo, Investor, and Team plans with Customer Portal support.
- **Entitlements Layer**: Implemented server-side gating and client hints for feature usage.
- **Onboarding Workflow**: Implemented Orient, Activate, and Reinforce steps for new users.
- **Support & Help Hub**: Fully searchable Help Center, in-app chat SLA logic, and Markdown-driven changelogs.

## Improved
- **Metrics Calculator Engine**: Faster computation speeds across our 10 primary financial metrics.
- **Luminous Glass Design System**: Enhanced backdrop-blur cards, responsive sidebar, and polished modal interfaces.

## Fixed
- **Authentication Handshake**: Fixed cross-origin partition issues for popup sign-ins.
