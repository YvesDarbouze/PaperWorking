What's your strategy for this property?

This shapes which workflows we set up for your project dashboard.

What's the difference between these? expand_less

FLIP

Short-term hold. You'll see Rehab and Exit screens. Occupancy and long-term cash flow are hidden because they don't apply.

RENTAL

Long-term hold. You'll see Operations, Tenants & Leases, and the full cash flow stack. Rehab is optional for light improvements.

BRRRR

Hybrid. You'll see Rehab during initial Hold, then Tenants & Leases and Operations after stabilization. Includes the refinance sub-phase.
