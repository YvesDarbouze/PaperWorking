## Issue #1 — Landing Page Hero Stats

**Current (failing):**
> `"Over $2.4B in capital tracked. 12,000+ active deals. Trusted by institutional syndicators nationwide."`

**Failed test**: Truth / Evidence

**Rewrite Option A:**
> `"Currently tracking 48 active deal pipelines across 12 markets in our pilot cohort. No false statistics. Just clean deal data."`

**Rewrite Option B:**
> `"Built by active real estate operators who got tired of spreadsheet errors. We track documents, dollars, and deadlines using verified calculations."`

**Recommendation**: Option B because it leverages the **StoryBrand** and **Cialdini Liking/Authority** frameworks. Real estate operators trust tools built by other practitioners who understand the exact pain points of running flips and syndications. Option B establishes authentic, non-manufactured authority.

**Frameworks applied**: StoryBrand (Guide authority), Cialdini (Liking, Authority), Show Don't Sell.
