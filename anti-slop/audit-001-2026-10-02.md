## 2. Findings List

### Finding F-001: Statutory Vehicle Coverage
- **Rule**: Product Architecture & Domain Fidelity (R-26)
- **Priority**: Verified / Closed
- **Observation**: User required dedicated, structured 4-task execution engines for:
  1. 🏢 **Developed Condo Sales** (Declaration & Plat Filing, HOA Launch, POS Delivery, Phased Sales Closings).
  2. 🏛️ **Developed Co-op Sales** (Form Housing Corp, Board Acceptance Rules, Offering Plan Clearance, Share Allocation & Board Package Review).
  3. 🔑 **Traditional Rental / 1031 Exchange** (Financial Stabilization / T12, Lease Audits & Estoppels, Tenant Notices & Escrow Transfer, 45/180-Day Identification & Replacement Closing).
  4. 📜 **Lease-Option / Rent-to-Own** (Lease-Option Agreement, Option Premium Escrow & Rent Credits, Maintenance Covenants, Purchase Trigger & Underwriting).
  5. 💵 **Straight Sale / Flip** (Full Waterfall, Realized Sale Net Proceeds, LP/GP Distribution).
  6. 🔄 **Refinance & Retain** (Cash-out Facility, Ongoing Active Hold KPIs).
- **Resolution**: Fully implemented and validated across both the conversational step engine (`ExitConversationalEngine.tsx`) and the executive workspace (`ExitStrategyExecutionCard.tsx`).

### Finding F-002: Inherited Fund Phase Baseline Handover
- **Rule**: Flow Continuity & Data Grounding (R-26)
- **Priority**: Verified / Closed
- **Observation**: Transition from Fund to Exit required verified financial handover (Closing Date, Purchase Price, Loan Facility, Closing Costs, Cash Invested, Holding Costs) without re-prompting or duplicate entry.
- **Resolution**: Implemented `FundHandoverCostBaseline` schema and visual indicator banner in `ExitConversationalEngine.tsx` and `ExitWorkspaceView.tsx`, displaying exact baseline cost figures inherited from the completed Fund phase.

### Finding F-003: Portfolio 33 KPIs Integration
- **Rule**: Complete Data Pipeline Integration (R-26)
- **Priority**: Verified / Closed
- **Observation**: Realized exit outcomes must feed directly into the 33 Portfolio KPIs.
- **Resolution**: Verified `apps/api/src/lib/reports/aggregation.ts` where exit data dynamically updates:
  - `totalExitRevenue`
  - `avgNetProfitPerDeal`
  - `annualizedROIPct`
  - `totalCapitalGains`
  - `exchange1031RatePct`
  - `avgDaysOnMarket`
  - `saleToListRatioPct`
  - `cashOnCashReturnPct` and `capRatePct`
  Furthermore, `apps/api/src/lib/exit/complete.ts` and `apps/api/src/routes/exit/complete/handler.ts` accept all 6 disposition strategies.
