# Data Room & Document Vault

The Data Room is a secure repository for all files, purchase agreements, appraisals, entity documents, and closing packages associated with your portfolio.

## Key Features

- **Document Folders**: Structurally grouped folders for LOIs, Due Diligence, Financing, and Entity records.
- **Drag-and-Drop Upload**: Simply drag files into the luminous glass upload zone to safely store them.
- **Secure Access Sharing**: Grant granular folder-level permissions for CPAs, lenders, or team members.
