# Billing & Subscriptions

Configure plans and payment details on PaperWorking.

## Plans Tiers

- **Solo ($39/mo)**: Mapped to 1 active project capacity, basic underwriting sheets.
- **Investor ($89/mo)**: Unlimited projects, portfolio rollups, and Deal Compare Board.
- **Team ($199/mo)**: Full team collaboration, priority support, and API access.

## Portal & Checkout

Manage invoices, update card details, or cancel plans anytime using the secure Stripe Customer Portal.
