# Projects Directory

The Projects page lists all properties and active development deals. You can initialize your first project, search/filter existing ones, and navigate into phase-specific workspaces.

## Managing Projects

- **Create a New Project**: Click "New Project" in the top right. This starts the onboarding wizard to input address, capital stack, and initial targets.
- **Phase Transition**: Every project is structured into 4 sequential phases:
  1. *Acquisition* (Sourcing & Screening)
  2. *Fund* (Due diligence & Closing checklist)
  3. *Hold* (Rehab & Construction tracking)
  4. *Exit* (Net Proceeds & Settlement ledger)
- **Status Pills**: Keep track of current project status, such as active, under contract, closed, or archived.
