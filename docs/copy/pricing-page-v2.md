## 4. Objection-Handling FAQs

### Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
- **A**: Yes. You can change your plan at any time in your **Settings**. If you upgrade, we will prorate the difference. If you downgrade, your account will be credited for the next billing cycle.

### Q: Is there any discount for paying annually?
- **A**: Yes, paying annually saves you 20% compared to paying month-to-month. The savings are automatically applied at checkout.

### Q: What is a "Read-Only Partner Seat"?
- **A**: Read-only seats let you invite external stakeholders—like passive investors, private lenders, or your spouse—to view project metrics and documents without being able to edit or delete any data.

### Q: Do you charge setup fees?
- **A**: No. There are no setup fees, contract minimums, or hidden charges. You pay only the flat monthly or annual fee for your chosen plan.
